Burning prograde raises the opposite side of the orbit (apogee); burning retrograde lowers it (perigee). Too much braking → the satellite re-enters the atmosphere. Orbital speed also naturally varies in an elliptical orbit.

Directional burn

Heading 0° = north, 90° = east. Commands travel through the simulated ground proxy.

📍 Go to location (lat / lon)

Enter a position and press “Go there”. The satellite plans a transfer (wait → burn → burn), flies over that point and settles into a stable circular orbit. A LEO satellite cannot hover over one spot – it keeps passing over it once per orbit.
